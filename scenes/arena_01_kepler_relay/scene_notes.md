# Arena 1 — Kepler Relay (Scene Notes)

## Theme & Mood
- **Setting**: High-tech planetary relay outpost built on metallic catwalks with industrial conduits and comms arrays.
- **Color Palette**: Dark slate grey metal, industrial yellow hazard striping, cool cyan energy emitters.
- **Atmosphere**: Active comms relay humming with energy before alien infestation.

## Key Objectives
- Clear initial waves of Melee Rift Stalkers and Ranged Spitters.
- Charge and activate the central Rift Beacon to initiate teleportation to Arena 2 (Sundered Rift).
