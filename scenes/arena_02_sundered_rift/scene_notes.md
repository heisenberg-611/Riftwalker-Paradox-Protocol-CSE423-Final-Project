# Arena 2 — Sundered Rift (Scene Notes)

## Theme & Mood
- **Setting**: Shattered alien astral void with floating crystalline islands suspended in deep space.
- **Color Palette**: Deep void purple, obsidian obsidian rock, eerie neon magenta and toxic green bioluminescence.
- **Atmosphere**: Cosmic rift disturbance with swirling astral dust and low-gravity floating platforms.

## Key Objectives
- Defend against aggressive swarms of elite alien variants.
- Defeat the **Rift Guardian Boss** to seal the temporal anomaly and win the game.
