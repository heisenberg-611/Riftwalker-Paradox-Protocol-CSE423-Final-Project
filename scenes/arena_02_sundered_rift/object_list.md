# Arena 2 — Sundered Rift (Object List)

1. **Arrival Rift Beacon**:
   - Resonant beacon linked to Kepler Relay.
2. **Floating Void Asteroids / Islands**:
   - Irregular polygonal floating terrain platforms with low gravity indicators.
3. **Alien Monoliths & Spire Crystals**:
   - Glowing hexagonal purple crystal spires (`glMaterialfv` emission and specular highlights).
4. **Boss Rift Core**:
   - Pulsing rift sphere at the north edge that summons the Rift Guardian.
