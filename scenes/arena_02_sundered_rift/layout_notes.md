# Arena 2 — Sundered Rift (Layout Notes)

## Dimensions
- Void Bounds (X): `-80.0` to `+80.0` (Total width: 160 units)
- Void Bounds (Z): `-80.0` to `+80.0` (Total length: 160 units)
- Main Island Floor (Y): `0.0`
- Floating Outer Shards (Y): `-5.0` to `+15.0`

## Key Zones
1. **Primary Bastion Island (`(0, 0, 0)`)**: Central obsidian battleground where the Rift Guardian descends.
2. **Arrival Beacon Shard (`(0, 0, -50)`)**: The landing platform where the player emerges after teleportation.
3. **East Crystal Spires (`(50, 0, 0)`)**: Floating alien crystal clusters.
4. **West Energy Nexus (`(-50, 0, 0)`)**: Rift rift vents providing Chrono energy recharging.
5. **North Void Altar (`(0, 0, 50)`)**: Boss spawning core.
